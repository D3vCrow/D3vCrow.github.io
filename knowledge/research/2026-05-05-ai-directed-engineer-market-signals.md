## 4 steal-worthy patterns

### 1. Six-dimensions framework (Augment Code)

Clean section headers / pillar overlay. Each dimension has a 1-line core question.

| Dimension | Core Question |
|---|---|
| Product & Outcome Taste | Are we building the right thing? |
| System & Architectural Judgment | Will this survive production? |
| Agent Leverage | Can you turn AI into real engineering throughput? |
| Communication & Collaboration | Can you reach clarity fast? |
| Ownership & Leadership | Do you drive outcomes, not just tasks? |
| Learning Velocity | Can you evolve as fast as the tools? |

Reusable phrases: "author shifted to architect and editor" · "agent leverage" · "real engineering throughput" · "the fastest teams reach clarity the fastest"

### 2. The 70/30 split (Osmani)

> "AI for grunt work, human for oversight and creative problem solving — maximize the human 30%, let AI handle the rote 70%."

Pairs with: **"Every engineer is a manager now"** + **"ultimate guarantor"** + **"Trust but verify"**.

Osmani's 6 best practices (numbered in essay):
1. Craft clear, contextual prompts (treat prompting as core skill, maintain personal prompt library)
2. Always review and verify AI outputs ("trust, but verify")
3. Manage scope (amplify, don't autopilot; clear boundaries for agents)
4. Continue learning and stay updated
5. Collaborate and establish team practices
6. Use AI responsibly and ethically

Plus an 8-step onboarding ladder for engineers new to AI-native work.

### 3. Orchestration-not-execution (Ishir)

Clean re-frame for "AI-Directed" away from "uses AI tools" (AI-First / co-pilot user) toward "AI is the default execution layer".

| Frame | AI-First | AI-Native (≈ AI-Directed) |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Executor + AI user | Orchestrator + system designer |
| Stance | "Uses AI as co-pilot" | "AI is the default execution layer" |
| Value | Speed and efficiency | Scalability and leverage |
| Mental model | "How do I build this?" | "How should AI build, test, and maintain this?" |

AI-Directed (Volaris JD) sits closer to AI-Native than AI-First.

### 4. Concrete portfolio markers (Agentic-Eng-Jobs + Buteau)

- **CLAUDE.md as named public artifact** — recruiters look for this
- **"Five workstreams in parallel"** — only hard market number found
- "Output per unit of human time" not velocity
- "System improvers, not just fast producers" (Buteau)
- "AI makes output volume a less reliable performance signal" (Buteau)
- Production deployments, not demos
- Failure analysis / agent-drift war stories as evidence
- Context engineering artifacts (CLAUDE.md, system prompts, ADRs) publicly visible

- **Ishir bottom half** = recruiter SEO filler (services pitch, geographic delivery list, FAQ block). Unsourced stat: "Skills evolve 66% faster than traditional roles" — do not quote.
- **Augment Code** = vocab gold, KPI desert. No hard numbers or audit cadences. Numbers must come from elsewhere (or Chris's own data).
- **Generic Indeed/x0pa "AI Engineer JD" templates** = off-target. These are AI/ML model-building roles, not "AI-directed work" roles. Different category.
