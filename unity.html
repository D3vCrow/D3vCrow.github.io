Studio

Pyles Studio

In 2020, my good friend Dimitris (now also my best man) saw his sculpting work disappear due to COVID. He pivoted into 3D art. Since we both loved games, we teamed up. That's how Pyles Studio was born.

Before long, others started joining us, some looking for experience, others for fun, all brought together by a shared passion for game development. A few of them now work at Blizzard and Creative Assembly.

Over 3.5 years and more than 5000 hours, it became our foundation. We learned by building, breaking, fixing, and most of all, having fun during the process.

2020 – 2024, In Development

Thrion Arenas

Unity URP Solo Developer Turn-Based FMOD PlayFab Cinemachine

Turn-based arena strategy with 4 playable champions, each with distinct ability sets. The systems: modular additive-scene architecture, object pooling, fog of war, inventory and loot systems, Cinemachine camera rigs, FMOD audio integration, and a full PlayFab backend handling auth, stats, and player progression. Every system written in C#, owned end-to-end. The arena prototype that became Pyles Studio's foundation.

In Development

Island Project

Unity HDRP Open World Burst Bakery FMOD Pyles Studio

Open-world survival lab in Unity HDRP. Custom Verlet-rope fishing line, event-driven thirst system, and Burst-compiled character mechanics holding push physics, animator layer blending, and pickup state in one class. Singleton typology (DDOL / Dominant / Regulator) for scene-spanning state. Bakery lighting, FMOD audio. Dimitris built the island, inspired by stories from our grandparents.

Solo Lab

DevCrow Experiments

My first real experience with design came over 20 years ago. My friends and I had finished every quest in our favorite board game, Hero Quest. So I asked if they wanted me to make a new one. They said yes. I spent a few days drawing maps, writing the story, adding puzzles, traps, side quests, anything I could think of. We played it in one sitting. No one wanted to stop.

In January 2025, I challenged myself to build and release a full game in just 17 days. Our twin daughters were only 3 months old, and life was, at best, chaotic. But I made it happen.

2025 – Active

Hack&Slash

Unity 6.2 Combat Lab Animation Rigging Timeline + Signals Input System

Active third-person combat lab in Unity 6.2. Radial-drag combo selector: mouse-drag picks an attack from a queue, the cooldown ring fills as you spend it. Camera-pitch aim offset via MultiAimConstraint keeps the rig pointed where you look. Boss phase transitions driven by Unity Timeline + Signal ScriptableObjects.
